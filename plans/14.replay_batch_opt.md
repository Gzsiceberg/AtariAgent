# Replay Batch Optimization

- Keep self-play, replay storage, batch sampling, training, logging, and checkpoints in the main process.
- Construct replay batches synchronously with vectorized, preallocated NumPy arrays and expose them with `torch.from_numpy`.
- Do not use Ray actors, shared-memory queues, prefetching, or concurrent priority updates.
- Local benchmark: replay construction fell from about 43.9 ms to 12.8 ms, reaching about 21.9 updates/s.
